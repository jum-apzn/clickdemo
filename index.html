    YOUR DEMO, OUT IN THE WORLD

    From screens to story.

    1. Add PNG, JPG or WebP screenshots, or try the example.
    2. Write a headline and caption for each screen. Click the image to place its next-step hotspot.
    3. Reorder with the arrows. Resize targets with the slider.
    4. Preview, then export a standalone HTML demo. Reopen your exported HTML or JSON to edit later. Replace screenshots without rewriting your story.

    Everything is processed in this browser. This version does not auto-save: download your project before closing. Exported files contain your screenshots, so check them for private information before sharing.
